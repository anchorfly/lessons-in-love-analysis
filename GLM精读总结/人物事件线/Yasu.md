## 四、与主线/元叙事的咬合点（核心价值）
1. **"the stars are not real"**（[2018]）：
   > ya: The stars are not real. They are the residue of hopes and wishes. Dreams and desires.
   角色宣告星空"不真实"——与 Wakana "Nothing is real"、Futaba/Sekai "not real" 同构，是"世界虚构性"母题的又一角色级表达。
2. **"tug of war...begin again with a brand new rope"**（[2811] / [2838]）：
   > ya: There is a third element to this game of tug of war.
   > ya: Would the game simply begin again...with a brand new rope?
   以 **"拔河游戏→用新绳重开"** 隐喻 **循环的终结与重启**——"第三元素""新绳"指向 cycle 结束后以新参数重启（呼应 Tsuneyo "cycle is about to end"、Yuki "last reset"）。
3. **Ami + Maya 共有书藏**（[1079] / [1683]）：
   > f: Ami and Maya even brought in most of their collection for you to sort through...
   > mo: ...Maya reads. PunPun isn't really entrance level material...
   明确 **Ami 与 Maya 共享一整批书藏**（含暗黑向《PunPun》），将两人绑定为"阅读/知识/母题"共同体，与 Ami 母 "voices/poems"、Maya 的诗歌密码同源。
4. **"Just don't tell that to Maya or she'll get sad"**（[913] / [1570]）：
   > N: Just don't tell that to Maya or she'll probably get sad and reflective.
   叙述者以 "don't tell Maya" 为默认谨慎——印证 Maya 对信息/真相的脆弱与掌控欲（与 Uta 线 Maya 情报战、Noriko 线 Maya 敌意一致）。
5. **"Blessed be you, Akira"**（[2739]）：宗教化使用 **Akira** 全名，真名在 Yasu 的信仰语境中被神圣化。
