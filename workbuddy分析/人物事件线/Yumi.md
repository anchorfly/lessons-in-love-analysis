## 四、与主线/元叙事的咬合点（核心价值）
1. **"我知道这游戏怎么运作"**（[2518]）：
   > y: They'll just think I'm trying to damage your reputation or some shit. I know how this game works.
   角色正面宣称"懂游戏的运作规则"——核心第四面墙/元认知。
2. **"Patreon friendly version" 自指**（[309]）：
   > y: ...punching old people is a character trait I have in the Patreon friendly version of the game.
   以"Patreon 版本"口吻描述自身行为——将角色自身指认为受版本/审查约束的内容。
3. **Maya 人格出现裂痕**（[3604]）：
   > N: Even that part of Maya's personality has been beginning to show cracks as of late.
   叙述者确认 **Maya 的人格/权威正在出现裂痕**——与 Tsuneyo 线"cycle is about to end...Maya can feel it"、Makoto 线"Maya 不再全能"互为印证。
4. **"巨大的墙" = 屏障**（[5085]）：
   > y: Can't. We've got that huge ass wall now, remember?
   与 Touka 线"outer barrier / isolation policy"、Ayane 未来线"Kumon-mi 屏障被拆除"一致——**世界被一道墙/屏障包围**是角色共识的物理事实。
5. **记忆模糊/既视感**（[986]–[988] "I remember kissing/hugging Yumi but...such a hazy memory it almost doesn't feel real"）：与全局循环记忆残缺母题一致。

## 四′、二轮增补（chap4part2 精读新证据，2026-08-22）

6. **pareidolia 寄宿脑内（关键新证据）**（dormwarssix9，源 3582–3612）：Yumi 赢下魔法赛后，一段斜体旁白直接在她脑内说话：
   > N: {i}told you it would work. now speak to the weird fucking turtle thing so we can get out of here and go back to being best friends.{/i}
   > y: ...Because you live inside of my fucking {i}head,{/i} maybe?
   > N: {i}or maybe you just like me — and you look forward to the way i think for you so you don't have to think for yourself.{/i}
   该声音还掌握 Yumi 的私密处境："not knowing whether your mother will live or die. not knowing where you and chika stand as friends."（[3591]）——**pareidolia（USER3）不仅寄宿 Yumi 脑内，且以"替她思考/代她生活"自居**；Yumi 当场向 Wise Turtle 祈求 "could you use your mysterious turtle powers to vaporize Pareidolia, please?"（[3594]）——Yumi 知道这个寄生物的名字。
7. **Wise Turtle 开口**（源 3595–3612）：Yumi 许愿后石龟竟回答 "Such a feat is beyond my power. But I will grant you victory and thank you for the affection you show my kind."——Yumi 与脑内声音同步惊呼 "{i}holy fuck.{/i}"，随后互问 "We didn't just mutually imagine that, did we?"／"we might have. shit's so weird around here..."——**元层道具首次应答角色**，且旁白（pareidolia）与 Yumi 共享同一惊吓。
8. **随口施法获胜**（源 3563–3568）：对 Molly 多年苦练的咒语，Yumi 以一句 "Abra kadabra." 直接取胜——她的"魔法"不需体系，暗示其存在层级本就高于常规角色（与"how Yumi was made"之谜呼应）。
