# Sara 事件线全析

> 源文件：`游戏文本/SaraEvents.rpy`（全文件共 38 个 label，从 `callsaraafternoon` 到 `dormwarssixsara1`）
> 定位：Sakakibara 家的母亲线——一个被原生家庭暴力、未成年师生关系与丧子之痛层层压弯的成年女性，如何在酒吧倒闭危机、女儿"步我后尘"的恐惧与元叙事凝视下，一步步走进母女共侍一人的结局。
> 阅读提示：台词直引格式为 `> 前缀: 英文原文`。sar 为 Sara，sa 为 Sana，h 为 Haruka，yu 为 Yuki，ni 为 Niki，a 为 Ami，ay 为 Ayane，maki 为 Maki，fat/moth/him 为回忆中的父亲、母亲与旧日教师，q 为不知名少女；无前缀者为旁白（N）。其中 `saraspring3`、`saraspring4` 两段的无前缀旁白是 Sara 的第一人称内心独白。
> 源文中大量 `if bonus:` 双分支，本文只把 bonus == True 一侧当作事实依据。另有少数按进度而非按版本切分的分支（如 `sarasex`、`saraspecial30p2`、`bar50`），文中均注明其触发条件。

## 一、角色基本盘

Sara 是 Sana 的生母，也是 Kumon-mi 一家酒吧的老板。母女关系由她本人在 `dormwarssixsara1` 里说破：`> sar: I carried her inside of me for nine months just so you could treat her like a toy?` 这家店没有正式店名，Sensei 戏称它为 "Sakaki-bar-a"，Sara 自己的解释是 `> sar: This building is quite literally built on top of a terrible pun.`（`saraspring1`）。她的核心设定由三层创伤叠成。

其一，未成年时与自己的高中教师发生关系并怀孕。她自述 `> sar: I was barely a freshman when I started sleeping with mine.`（`saradate10`，bonus == True 分支）；Sana 在 `saraspring6` 里直接点破同一件事：`> sa: I'm the same age you were when {i}you{/i} were having unprotected sex with your teacher.`——这两处把 Sana 的父亲与那位教师指向同一人。在 `sarabar25p2` 里她给出一句冷酷的统计：`> sar: Well, based on {i}my{/i} personal experience, every high school teacher I've ever had has fucked me.`（bonus == True 分支。）`saradate10` 里 Sensei 追问对方是不是滥用身份的人（`> s: I take it he was one of those people who abused his position?`），她答 `> sar: Will you think I'm dumb or gullible if I say he was?`（bonus == True 分支。）男方在她怀孕后抛下母女俩离开，`sarabar25` 里她这样算账：`> sar: it's one thing to knock up a teenage girl, but to {i}abandon{/i} her and force her to be a single parent before she can even legally rent a car?` 这构成了她对所有关系的底层预期——`> sar: People leave me, Sensei. It's what they do.`（`sarabar25p2`）

其二，原生家庭暴力。`saraspring3` 里有一段完整的回溯：平日晚十点是她的门禁，超过就要挨打（`> N: I was supposed to be back by 10:00 on weeknights or my father would hit me.`）。那天她晚归，父亲借"碗没洗"发难，母亲则拿她身上的香水味做文章，指控她偷了自己的 No.5。争执中父亲扇她耳光、掐住她的脖子，母亲只在旁边说 `> moth: Don't choke the girl, dear. Even {i}if{/i} she deserves it.`；更早前母亲已落下那句家训：`> moth: If you get knocked up, make sure it's a rich boy. We're tired of running that shitty old bar your grandfather left us.` 父亲酗酒（`> sar: But you're always too fucking drunk to-`），并把自己父亲的行径当作尺度：`> fat: That was five seconds. {i}My{/i} dad used to strangle me until my face turned blue.` 收场是门禁被提前到九点。这段经历直接塑造了她的育儿观——`> sar: I didn't have much of a childhood.` `> sar: So when it came time to raise...children of my own, I just did whatever I wished would have been done for me when I was their age.`（`sarabar25p2`）以及 `saraspring3` 末尾那句自我推翻：`> N: I used to think I was a better parent than mine because {i}I{/i} never put my children through the Hell that {i}I{/i} was put through.` `> N: But then one of them found it on accident.`

其三，一个死去的儿子。`sarabar30` 的电影之夜，Sara 在 Haruka 为她编头发时忽然说出 `> sar: I miss my son.`，并请求 `> sar: Can I...` `> sar: Talk about him for a little while?...`；话未出口 Sana 推门进来，她立刻把话题转成"要不要帮妈妈编辫子"（`> sar: Do you want to help braid my hair?...`）。`saracamp1` 从 Sensei 视角补上旁证：`> N: The way she can smile like that after losing a son is something I can only hope to emulate.` 而最完整的一次交代出现在 `saraspring3` 的独白里：`> N: My son became a ghost in my place.` `> N: Now I struggle to even bring him up without everything around me shattering into a million pieces.`

性格层面，她是"少女心被困在母亲身体里"的存在。旁白直接给出这个定义（`saragenafternoon`，bonus == True 分支）：`> N: She's like a [teenage]girl trapped in an attractive mother's body...which might actually explain why I like hanging out with her as much as I do.` 她嗜甜——`saradate1` 里两人在城里逛了约一小时，沿途进便利店给她买糖（`> N: ...stopping at a few convenience stores along the way to buy Sara candy.`）；她爱 boba，并且会"假装"忘带钱包让 Sensei 付账（`saragenafternoon`：`> N: She then {i}pretends{/i} to forget her wallet and I have to pay for her.`）；`sarabar20` 里她还自陈常去女仆咖啡厅吃午饭，理由是价格便宜、女孩可爱（`> sar: Oh, I go there for lunch sometimes. The prices are great and the girls are adorable.`）

与此同时，她是源文中明确与 Sensei 共享同类症状的人。`saraspecial30p2` 里她系统描述过：房间角落里的影子、用听不懂的语言跟她说话的身影、整段时间凭空消失、在陌生地方醒来而不知自己如何到的那里。她对自己的概括只有两个字：`> sar: Broken.` `> sar: We both are.`

## 二、love 线逐事件脉络

**saradate1（初次约会）**。Sensei 用她某晚写在收据上的电话号码约她出门（`> N: I just plugged hers into my phone after she wrote it on a receipt that one night`）。他临时上网查了 Kumon-mi 市区的地标，挑了一栋看起来很大的楼当见面点（`> N: I take a few minutes to research notable locations in the urban district of Kumon-mi`），到场才看清是某家手机公司的总部（`> N: I turn to the side to see that we're in front of the headquarters for some unfamiliar cell phone company.`）。围绕 5G 与 6G 的荒诞调情之后（`> sar: I can tell. I'm so horny now. Please make love to me beneath the giant 5G banner towering over us.` `> sar: If it's for you, [saramaster], I'd let you do me under {i}6G{/i}.`），Sara 迅速推进关系：`> sar: Wow. We've been together almost a full hour and you're not bored of me yet.` `> sar: Maybe we {i}are{/i} meant to be together after all.` 被问到要不要更进一步时她说 `> sar: Like I could be your girlfriend. Or something.`；被 Sensei 以"太快了"推回去后，她自己把话题带向"亲戚"：`> sar: We both have other people that are important to us that we should focus on.` `> sar: I have my daughter...You have your [niece]...` `> sar: And I can't imagine they'd feel normal about becoming related all of a sudden.` 事件后半段转入正题：Sara 承认酒吧撑不下去了——`> sar: The place is going downhill fast and I'm not really sure how long I can keep going at this rate.` `> sar: We were fine until all of the men started disappearing, but now it's like we're imploding or something!` 她向完全外行的 Sensei 讨经营建议，最后的方案是 `> sar: Can you at least keep coming by and spending lots of money then?`，并当场封他 `> sar: You're officially my favorite customer!` 这次约会确立了 Sara 的追求模式：直球、越界、以孤独为底。

**sarainvite1（邀请来家·初见 Ami）**。Sara 第一次进 Sensei 的家。进门五分钟内话题就滑向同居（`> sar: It looks like the two of us would have to order out quite often if we ever moved in together.` `> s: You've been here for five minutes.`）。Ami 回来前，她对着门锁的声音整理仪容，被 Sensei 问为什么更在意侄女的观感时说出本事件最关键的一句：`> sar: I already know you like me. I need to make her like me next and I'll be basically guaranteed a spot in this family.`（bonus == True 分支。）Ami 进屋后的反应是 `> a: I think I might have opened a door to the wrong dimension.` `> a: I'm going to leave and come back in and I'm sure everything will go back to normal.` Sara 随即用一记精准的赞美攻势瓦解敌意——她称 Sensei 邀请她时说的是"我要向你证明我侄女是全 Kumon-mi 最可爱的女孩"（`> sar: In fact, when he invited me over, he specifically said, "I need to prove to you that my [niece] is the cutest girl in all of Kumon-mi."`），然后当着 Ami 的面承认自己输了：`> sar: I...I think I might have to agree.`；紧接着又加一句 `> sar: You take care of your [uncle] too, don't you? That's very impressive for a girl your age.`（bonus == True 分支。）旁白对此的评价是 `> N: This is a dangerous can of worms you're opening, Sara.` 收尾时两个女人已经约好把 Sensei 排除在外的"女生之夜"：`> sar: In fact, what if we leave Sensei out of it entirely and have a little girls' night in?`，并把话题一路聊到天黑：`> N: The two of them carry on their conversation and ignore me all the way until night falls.` 但他的结论反而是 `> N: Even though the two of us didn't get to spend much time actually {i}talking{/i} to one another, I still feel like I know Sara a lot better than I did before...`

**sarainvite2（第二次邀请·红绳断裂分支）**。Ami 不在家。若此前尚未与 Sara 发生关系（`sarasex == False`）且 bonus == True，Sara 会吐露上次被拒后的羞耻：`> sar: That really wasn't my finest moment, you know.
