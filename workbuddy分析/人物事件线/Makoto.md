## 一、角色基本盘

- **身份**：学生（与 Maki 同名女儿无直接关系，但设定上 Maki 称"我女儿叫 Makoto"制造递归玩笑）， Sensei 的"protege/门生"（[3412] "I'm a bit of a protege to you"）；在 Maki 的成人用品店打工。
- **表面性格**：傲娇、以"霸凌 Sensei"为乐（[6465] "I'm the one who's supposed to be bullying him now"）、嘴硬心软；是"sadgirls"群像成员（sadgirls1/7/8）。
- **深层状态（核心）**：**Makoto 保留跨循环记忆**——她记得"上一次迭代的自己"、记得与 Sensei "在卧室发生的事"却不知何时（[2549]/[2552]），并因此被归入 Maya/Ayane 的"觉醒者"行列。
- **关键变量**：makoto_love / makoto_lust；bonus 分支。

## 四、与主线咬合点（核心）

1. **跨循环记忆 / "上一次迭代的自己"**（[3545]）：旁白 "I have no idea what sort of person the previous iteration of me was, but if he wasn't attracted to Makoto...was he even really a person at all?"——明确使用 "previous iteration"（上一次迭代）指认 past-loop 自我，是全书最直白的 loop 自觉表述之一；Makoto 本人也 [2549] "I...remember it happening. But I don't remember when."、[2552] 记得"与 Sensei 在卧室发生的事"。
2. **"this version of the game"**（[1623]）：旁白 "I console her because I am a little nicer in this version of the game."——将当前循环称为"这个版本的游戏"，与 Kaori 线 connor、Miku 线 Selebus、Maki 线 [2336] 同构。
3. **Makoto 调查 Maya ↔ Akira 关联**（[2883]–[2884]）：mak "I also looked into Maya as well because...I was curious about their apparent connection with one another. Maya spends a good deal of time at your house, correct?"——Makoto 主动侦查 Maya 与 Sensei 的关系，证明她以"元认知"姿态观察主角圈。
4. **与 Maya、Ayane 并列的特殊三人组**（[6878]）：Sensei "I'm talking about how this shouldn't be possible with anyone other than Maya and Ayane...and yet here you are, perfectly navigating the talk as if it's something you've known all along."——叙述者将 Makoto 与 Maya、Ayane 并列，指认三者同属"本不可能却仿佛早已熟知一切"的觉醒存在。这直接呼应 Ayane 线 "Rooftop Apocalypse Squad" 觉醒者网络。
5. **"游戏里的女孩"双关**（[286]/[1108]/[1122]）：makotoinvite [286] "how I'd bang every single girl in the game she's playing"；[1108] "Patreon doesn't want this scene in the game."；[1122] "she hasn't even shown up in the game yet."——Makoto 玩法上"在玩一款游戏"，而该游戏与《Lessons in Love》形成镜像，构成"游戏套游戏"嵌套。
6. **母亲之死的"not real"噩梦**（[4783]）：mak "Mom! Tell me it's not real! Tell me this is all just a nightmare!"——Makoto（此处似为 Maki 线语境的母女场景）喊出"这不是真实"，与 Miku [8454]、Futaba [10374] 的"not real"母题同构；[6085] "Makoto will suffer as she remembers the life of the man she loved most" 指向她记得"所爱之人的前世"。
