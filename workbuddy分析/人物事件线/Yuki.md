# Yuki 事件线全析

> 源文件：YukiEvents.rpy ｜ 共 21 个 label（4 个分发/电话桩、1 个通用夜间事件、7 个 date 事件、2 个 camp 章、7 个 spring 章；另有 1 个露营选择菜单 `campmenu1`）
> 定位：Kumon-mi 成年人世界的核心样本——Yumi 的生母、前少女团伙成员、康复期成瘾者、癌症患者；经由 Tsubasa（Nee-sama）接入城市权力顶层，是全局里少数横跨底层与顶层的角色。她的线表面是「教师与学生母亲的忘年恋」，实质是一部用粗口包裹的死亡叙事：从戒断、复吸到确诊、乞钱、受辱、和解，每个 date label 都在推进同一个问题——一个自认该死的人如何学会想活。
> 阅读提示：英文台词均直引源文，紧跟的 label 名即其出处；本文件不标注行号，label 名是唯一锚点。人物代号：yu=Yuki、s=Sensei、yo=Yoshitsugu、tb=Tsubasa、sar=Sara、sa=Sana、ri=Rika、yumi=Yumi、y=Yumi（spring3 起）、k=Kaori、ima=Imani、t=Tsuneyo、a=Ami、h=Haruka、maki=Maki、na=Nao、N=旁白。

## 一、角色基本盘

- **身份**：Yumi 的生母，夫姓 Yamaguchi；婚前姓被叙述者主动悬置——*"Well, I guess Yuki's maiden name would be something different...but I'm not really in any rush to figure out whatever it was"*（`yukidate10p2`，旁白）。她在 Sara 的酒吧 Sakaki-bar-a 打工，住低收入公寓（`yukispring1`）；确诊后搬进 Tsubasa 的宅邸，由对方支付治疗费（`yukispring6`）。
- **家庭**：父母在乡下经营糖果店，她小时候在店里帮忙到十几岁（`yukidate1`）；高中时以「这镇子太无聊，我得离开」为由离家，加入了一个少女团伙（`yukidate1`、`yukidate5`）；与黑道成员 Yoshitsugu 结婚并生下 Yumi，后脱离，仍被他称为「我的妻子」（`yukispring5`）。侄女 Kaori 在万圣节事件后回到她的生活（`yukidate10` 开场旁白明言 Sensei 此前「revealed her long-lost and no longer comatose niece to her」）。
- **核心病理**：①数十年成瘾与戒断循环，自订 Rule #1——*"Things can {i}always{/i} get bad again...The day after that, somethin' could snap and I could wind up in a bag"*（`yukidate1`）；②自我厌恶：*"I'm a fuckin' deadbeat, dude"*、*"This is what I do"*（`yukispring1`）、*"I haven't felt good about myself in almost forty years"*（`yukispring1`）；③确诊后以自毁对抗恐惧——*"it's better for me to do it than a fucking SICKNESS"*（`yukispring3`）；④十一岁起吸烟，病灶在肺——*"I've been smoking since I was like eleven"*、*"It's in my lungs, Akira. Surprise, surprise."*（`yukispring3`）。
- **符号系统**：①**机车与风**——骑行是她整理思绪的仪式：*"Ridin' for me has always been a time to get my thoughts together. Everything always clogs up once I stop movin', so the wind helps rattle my brain and make shit clear again."*（`yukidate25`）；独白章里则回溯 *"She remembers the wind in her hair. - **人际网络**：向下连接 Yumi（修复中的母女）与 Kaori（托孤对象）；平行连接 Sara（雇主，且自称 "I'm pretty much the only one she's got"，`yukispring1`）、Tsuneyo（*"Only other person in Kumon-mi who knows outside of the fuckin' Yakuza is Tsu-chan"*，`yukidate5`）、Rika（主动上门示好却被她赶走的邻居，`yukispring2`）、Imani（隔壁邻居，在 `yukidate20p2` 走廊上撞见）；向上连接 Tsubasa（救命恩人与金主）；与 Sensei 构成情感主轴。

### camp 双章

**yukicamp1** Ami 露营旅行。开篇独白是 Sensei 对 Ami 的「塑形宣言」——*"Ami's still young. So, if I want to mold her world, I need to do it now...runs the risk of turning her into an actual monster who will pry open my ribcage before ripping my heart out and swallowing it whole."*，并以 *"Our time is numbered. This will almost certainly be fatal."* 定调。Yuki 骑车到场，初次正式认识 Ami，开口就替女儿致歉（*"Sorry if my daughter's ever picked on you before."*）。她把 Sensei 拉进树林，谈的是「父亲角色」（*"Sara tells me you're lookin' to be like more of a dad to Ami or some shit?"*），两位失职家长互诊。爱的定义交锋：Sensei——*"I think it comes down to how much pain you're willing to withstand to prevent that person from going through the same thing."*；Yuki 指出这个标准对他们这类人无效（*"you and me don't really give a fuck about ourselves"*），并直言自己愿为 Yumi 挨刀，也同样愿为 Sensei 挨刀，却并不爱他。惊悚插叙：Sensei 自述前几晚准备跳桥时被 Yumi 撞见（*"I ran into her the other night when I was about to jump off a bridge."*），Yuki 的反应是 "That's my girl."。结尾 Kaori 带着不会说话的 Nao 出现在林中，宣布自己在寻宝。事件以 `campmenu1` 交给玩家选择。

**yukicamp2** 河畔对饮，本线哲学浓度峰值。开篇旁白以德国食人案（Rotenburg / Armin Meiwes）为喻，讨论「不知自己喜不喜欢一个人」的进退两难，随后**直接对读者喊话**：*"If anyone is reading this or hearing this or watching this or whatever it is you're supposed to be doing or think that you're doing or are only doing because I think you're supposed to hear or read or listen, know this."*——元叙事玩家层的明牌。封闭城市设定借她之口补完：曾有欧洲客人进店，她是唯一会说英语的人（只会基础会话），师承 *"an old friend. One of those creative types, like you."*；Sensei 试探翻墙离城，她讥讽 *"Moment you doubt yourself is the moment you fall several hundred feet and turn into a fuckin' Sensei puddle."*。她的逃亡哲学直接否定「换个地方就能重新开始」：*"you'll move and you'll move and you'll move, but you'll never get any further away from the shit you're trying to escape from"*；解法唯有直面——*"Nobody can escape the past, man. All you can do is confront it."*。她也点破自己「停下」才是一切变化的起点（*"Shit didn't change until I {i}stopped.{/i}"*）。结尾她请求 Sensei 不要再当面说她可爱——因为 Sara 也喜欢他——并承认 *"This blows and I keep wishin' the fuckin' circumstances were different."*。love 主轴至此定格为「彼此知晓、彼此搁置」。

### spring 系列：崩塌与救援

**yukispring1** 干预事件。Sara 用随身多年的万能钥匙强行开门（钥匙来历被她亲手岔开：Sensei 问 "Why do you have that?"，她答 "Excellent question, Sensei." 随即径直进屋）。屋内 Yuki 烂醉昏睡、满屋酒罐；Sara 逐一排查针具毒品（*"Needles...Pipes...Small plastic baggies...You don't see any of that, do you?"*），并自曝出身酒鬼家庭（*"I come from a long line of people who drink more than they probably should."*；后文又说 "My parents were raging alcoholics"）。训斥戏里 Yuki 交出全线最重要的自我诊断：*"All my life, I've been runnin' from {i}somethin'{/i}. Home. Cops. Yakuza. Yumi. Drugs...For the first time ever, I ain't got that. Went from there always bein' something in the background I need to look out for to...my own couch. Buyin' TV dinners for two people instead of one. {i}Money.{/i} I don't fuckin' deserve any of that."*，继而否认神的存在：*"That's how I know there ain't no god. Fucker's fine with me doin' everythin' in my power to burn this world to the ground while lettin' the ones who could do some good rot."*。Sara 下最后通牒：*"You miss one more shift, you're done. Got it?"*。Sara 摔门而去后，Sensei 承认自己的愤怒源于照镜（*"I feel sick each time I look in the mirror — and my reflection isn't far from here."*）。

**yukispring2** 余波与 Rika 登场。Yuki 半承认复吸：*"Maybe. Maybe not. I can say whatever I want and you won't know what to believe."*，并在同一晚抛出 *"You might be the first guy I've ever actually had feelings for."*——在人生崩塌的时点而非甜蜜时点交付真心。Rika 携自制万圣节饼干闯入，Three 段信息在此交付：Yumi 的生日就是万圣节（Yuki 因而对这天没有好回忆）；Rika 时年 42 岁（旁白直陈 "Rika is 42."）；Yuki 展示背部未完成的 horimono，并解释那是「Dragon」的残骸。Rika 追问「有没有可能亲一个女孩」，Yuki 明确回绝（*"I ain't into chicks, Rika."*），最终把她赶走：*"There's already one happy-go-lucky girl dead set on makin' my life all exhausting and shit. I don't need another one."*。送客后的第一次临终嘱托：*"Just take care of Yumi if some shit ever happens to me, aight?"*。事件结尾是全线最刺的镜像：Sensei 回家后在屏幕蓝光下自渎（*"Illuminated by the blue glow of my computer monitor, I unzip and unbutton my jeans...I cum hard into a bundle of tissues as a silent video plays before me."*）——两个照顾不了自己的人互为对方的安慰剂。

## 三、lust 线概貌

本线**没有独立命名的 lust label**（源文件内不存在以 yuki 开头的 lust label，也没有 `yuki_lust` 计数），欲望内容全部内嵌于 love 事件与 Yuki 视角章：

1. **交换性关系的过去**（`yukidate5`）：与 Yumi 之父的关系本质是生存交换，且含她在嗑药失去意识时被施行的性侵——这段内容的功能不是色情而是病理报告：它一次性解释了她对男性的不信任、"unconscious" 成为择偶底线、以及她对 Sensei「不求回报的好」的震惊（*"Probably the first guy I've ever met who's done something for me without trying to get into my pants"*）。
