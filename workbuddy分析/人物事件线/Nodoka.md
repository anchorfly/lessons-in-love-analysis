## 四、与主线/元叙事的咬合点
1. **"你是作家/我等你回归游戏"自指**（[3343]）：
   > no: Or maybe I'm secretly a beloved fan of yours who's been following you ever since I was a little girl? Desperately awaiting the day her favorite writer returns to the game?
   角色将 Sensei 指认为 **writer / 创作者**，并说"等你回到游戏"——直接点出叙事层。
2. **"记住你正在读的东西"第四面墙**（[2338]）：
   > no: But to that I'd say- remember what you're reading.
   对"读者"说话，打破第四面墙。
3. **Akira 真名揭露**（[4033] / [4057]）：
   > o: Sensei's name is Akira and he's been hiding it from everyone for reasons I don't understand.
   > no: Akira?...  ／  no: Akira...Arakawa...
   Otoha 向 Nodoka 点破 **Akira** 真名，Nodoka 重复确认"Arakawa"。
4. **与 Maya 的圈层互动**（[2977]）：
   > a: If only Ayane was here today. She could have taken Yumi. Right, Maya?
   Nodoka 线收尾处 Ami 直接叫 **Maya** 名字，证明 Maya 活跃于该群像场景。
5. **" Someone who isn't real" 扮演母题**（[720]）：
   > no: ...imagining how I would feel as someone else...Someone who isn't real.
   与全局"角色是否真实/被创造"母题同构。
