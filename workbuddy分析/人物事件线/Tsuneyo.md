## 四、与主线/元叙事的咬合点（核心价值）
1. **"six: Turn off the game!"**（[2931]）：
   > six: Turn off the game!
   **six** 即 DormEvents `roomwithclocks` 中登场的 six/te/sev 三角色之一（系统/世界维护者），在此由 Tsuneyo 场景呼出"关掉游戏"——**将 Dorm 的元叙事角色直接接入角色线**，证明 six/te/sev 是跨事件可见的元层存在。
2. **"cycle is about to end...Maya can feel it"**（[3802]–[3824]）：
   > s: ...do you remember anything you said on the beach about how everything feels like it's moving in circles sometimes?
   > t: I do. Just as I remember how you said it actually *is.* ...
   > s: A cycle is about to end, apparently. *I* can't feel it, but Maya can. Maybe you're feeling the same thing?
   **全游戏最直白的循环终局宣告**：Sensei 亲口说出"一个 cycle 即将结束"，且 **Maya 能感知 cycle 的终结**——坐实 Maya 作为循环节点/感知者的核心地位（与 Tsukasa 线 "failsafe"、Yuki 线 "last reset" 一致）。
3. **"Maya 的 attack-hug 版本"**（[2243]）：
   > N: ...there aren't many other versions that, at the very least, Maya has attack-hugged following the end of a wintry beach trip.
   以"Maya 的多个版本/多次 attack-hug"暗示 **Maya 跨迭代重复同一行为**，印证 Maya 是循环的恒定锚点。
4. **屏障之墙**（[1642] 间接 / Yumi[5085] "huge ass wall"）：Tsuneyo 场景通过 Touka 关联"outer barrier"，世界被墙包围是共识。
